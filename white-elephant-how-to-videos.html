What the videos won't tell you

Tutorials cover the mechanics; the hard parts are the house rules. Before game night, settle the Three-Owner Rule lock, the no-immediate-steal-backs rule, and Player 1's final swap — then pick a game mode if your crowd wants chaos. The videos give you the grammar; the room writes the story.

Videos FAQ

What's the best video for learning white elephant rules?

Cameron Rhodes' "White Elephant Gift Exchange – a how to guide" is the most-watched tutorial and covers the full turn order in under three minutes. GiftList's chaptered explainer is the cleanest backup.

Do these videos cover Yankee Swap and Dirty Santa too?

The SolidRoots explainer covers all three names in one video, and the official Office clip is technically a Yankee Swap. Mechanics are the same; see our glossary for the differences.

Can I show these at my party?

Yes — the 2–3 minute tutorials are perfect to screen before the first turn so you're not answering rules questions mid-game.

Why feature pop-culture clips?

Because watching a room react to a steal — even a scripted one — teaches the emotional arc of the game better than any rules recap.
