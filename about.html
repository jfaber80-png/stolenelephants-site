About Stolen Elephants

Stolen Elephants is a guide to the white elephant gift exchange and an online game that runs it for you. It is made by BOBO WEB CO LLC, a small company in Lantana, Texas, doing business as Stolen Elephants.

What we make

Two things, built to work together:

  • The guide (this site): the rules, the regional names such as Yankee Swap and Dirty Santa, gift ideas by budget, and how to host at an office, in a classroom, with family or online. It is free to read.
  • The game at play.stolenelephants.com: it draws the turn order, tracks every steal, locks gifts under the Three-Owner Rule, and lets remote guests play alongside the room. It is free for up to 8 players; larger games are a one-time fee per event (see pricing).

How the guides are written

The rules pages describe how the game is actually played at parties and offices, including the common house rules and where groups disagree. Where a rule varies, we say so rather than pick one as the only right answer.

The gift lists start from what people report actually got stolen at their exchanges, then we check that each item is still sold. Prices are shown as price bands because store prices change daily.

Every page shows the date it was last updated. If something is wrong or out of date, email Support@stolenelephants.com and we will fix it.

Who is Ellie Phant?

Ellie Phant is our mascot and the voice of the game: she runs the steals, sends the game emails from ellie@stolenelephants.com and keeps score. Whether she is an actual white elephant has never been settled.

How we make money

From paid game plans (Party and Business) and from some gift links. Some product links go to Amazon and other stores with an affiliate tag: if you buy, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. As an Amazon Associate, we earn from qualifying purchases. Affiliate links never decide what goes on a list.

We don't sell personal data. See our Privacy Policy and Terms for details.
