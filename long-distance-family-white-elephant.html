Long-Distance Family White Elephant

The kids moved. The grandkids multiplied. Somebody's always in a hospital or an airport or a different country now. Stolen Elephants keeps the family's white elephant tradition alive with one shared pile — and lets every branch of the family tree steal from it.

One Pile · Five Time Zones

Quick answer

A long-distance family can keep its white elephant tradition with one shared pile. Pick a week instead of a day so each household plays on its own schedule; everyone ships a gift ahead; the app draws the order and tracks the steals; and the Three-Owner Rule ends disputes over how many times a gift moved.

The tradition, minus the travel: no flight required, no kitchen big enough for 22 people, no coordinating whose basement hosts. The pile lives in the app; the family lives wherever life put them; the screaming happens in the group chat. No gift yet? No panic. When you add your gift, just paste a link from an online retailer — Amazon, Target, or anywhere else — and the app turns it into a proper pile entry. Your recipient gets a direct link to their winning loot, not a mystery box they have to guess about.

How the family game runs across the map

Same skeleton as the kitchen-table classic, stretched to fit a map.

  1. Pick the week, not the day. Async events let each household take their turn when the kids are down and the timezone cooperates.
  2. Ship the gifts ahead. Wrap prices, not presents: everyone buys online and sends directly, or drops one gift at a designated shipper. The app tracks the pile; USPS tracks the boxes.
  3. The app draws the order. Grandkids first, grandpa last — or however your house amendments run it. Nobody argues with an app at Thanksgiving anymore.
  4. Turns fire, steals land, locks lock. The Three-Owner Rule ends the 'that's the FOURTH time this gift moved' dispute before it starts.
  5. The finale call is optional. One short video call for the last unwrap and Player 1's final swap — or skip it and read the chaos in the group chat.

The Yankee Swap version, for the purists

Same pile, different finale.

Families that play strict Yankee Swap — every gift owned by exactly one person at once, final swap for Player 1 — get the identical treatment virtually. The app tracks gift ownership through every steal and runs the last-player swap without anyone counting on their fingers. Async or live, the swap sequence is the same drama with a longer fuse.

Long Distance Family White Elephant FAQ

The questions that come up before the first steal.

How do we do a family white elephant when everyone lives far apart?

Run an async event: gifts ship directly to recipients, each household takes a turn during its own week, and the app tracks every steal and lock. One finale video call is optional.

Can a family member in the hospital participate?

Yes — spectator-plus mode lets them follow every steal live and take their real turns from a phone. The game comes to the bedside instead of waiting for the discharge.

Do gifts have to be shipped before the game starts?

Best practice, yes: buy online and ship directly, so the winner's prize is already en route when they unwrap. The pile is tracked in the app either way.

Does the Three-Owner Rule still work asynchronously?

Yes — the app enforces it in real time regardless of when each player takes their turn. Async steals lock gifts exactly like live ones, no honor system required.
