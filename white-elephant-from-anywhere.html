White Elephant From Anywhere

A hospital stay doesn't cancel the family game. A twelve-hour layover doesn't bench your teammate. Stolen Elephants lets anyone follow the action steal by steal — and still take a real turn, from a phone, from anywhere on Earth.

Bedside · Layover · Overseas

Quick answer

Anyone can take a real turn in a white elephant game from a phone, from anywhere. Stolen Elephants lets players follow every steal live and still unwrap or steal on their turn, built for situations like a hospital stay, an airport layover, or a time-zone gap, so no one is left watching as a spectator.

The whole point: being far away is not the same as being less present. A spectator-plus player sees every unwrap and every steal as it happens, and when their number comes up, they choose — steal the candle or unwrap the mystery. No proxy decisions, no 'we saved you the leftovers.' No gift yet? No panic. When you add your gift, just paste a link from an online retailer — Amazon, Target, or anywhere else — and the app turns it into a proper pile entry. Your recipient gets a direct link to their winning loot, not a mystery box they have to guess about.

The airport purgatory

LAYOVER

Stranded in Dallas with a three-hour delay? Take your turn from gate B12. Steal, unwrap, gloat, board the plane.

Turns are short on purpose: a minute on your phone is a complete turn, and the pile moves on without waiting for your boarding call.

The overseas posting

GMT+7

Stationed abroad or backpacking through a different hemisphere? The event's clock bends to your calendar, not the family's kitchen.

Async mode means the difference between your turn landing at 3 a.m. and landing at your 3 p.m. Either way, it's your turn — not your proxy's.

Follow the action steal by steal

Not a scoreboard. The actual drama.

A spectator-plus player gets the live event view: the pile, the turn order, every unwrap revealed as it happens, every steal chain as it resolves. Push notifications flag when it's their turn — and when the Three-Owner Rule locks the gift they had their eye on. It's the difference between watching the game and being in it, and it works for a Yankee Swap exactly the same way.

White Elephant From Anywhere FAQ

The questions that come up before the first steal.

Can someone in a hospital play a white elephant remotely?

Yes. They join from a phone browser, follow every steal live, and take their real turn when their number comes up — no proxy, no exceptions made on their behalf.

What if my turn comes while I'm on a plane or asleep?

In async mode the event waits for you within the host's turn window. Hosts can keep a strict cadence or let the game breathe across days; the pile and rules are identical either way.

Do remote players get ignored in virtual gift exchanges?

Not in this one. Spectator-plus exists so distant players see the same drama in real time and act on it — the design goal was exactly that nobody becomes an afterthought.

Can a player join late from another country?

Yes. The join link works from any browser, and a late arrival slots into the draw order the app has already published. Time zones are handled by async turns, not by asking twelve relatives to wake up early.
