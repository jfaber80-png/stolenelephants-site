Themed gift pools

White Elephant Themes

The rules make the game fair — the theme makes it unforgettable. Fifteen ways to constrain the gift pool, from regift chaos to hot-sauce roulette, each with invite wording you can steal.

Theme: bring your worst

Quick answer

A theme tells everyone what kind of gift to bring, so the pile looks curated and steals feel fair instead of random. The game mechanics never change; only the gift pool does. This page offers 15 themes, from regift chaos to hot-sauce roulette, each with invitation wording you can reuse.

Why theme a white elephant?

An open gift pool lives or dies on the room's shared expectations — half gag gifts next to half serious gifts and the steals feel random and unfair. A theme fixes the expectation in one word, makes the pile look curated, and gives everyone the same creative brief. The game mechanics never change: same numbered turns, same steal rules, same freeze. Only the pool transforms.

The 15 themes

1. Regift Chaos

Bring something you actually own and won't miss. Every gift has a history — owners confess after the game.

2. Dollar Store Mayhem

$10 cap, entire haul from the dollar store. Absurdity guaranteed; the best gifts get gloriously deranged.

3. Keep It Local

Only findable in your town: farmers' market goods, local roasters, team merch. Doubles as a tourism ad.

4. Homemade Only

Baked, canned, knitted, built. Highest sentimental-steal rate of any theme — grandma recipes cause family wars.

5. Ornament Exchange

Every gift is a tree ornament. Slow-burn steals and a tree that documents the party for years.

6. Raise a Glass

Wine, craft beer, fancy mixers, glassware. Adults-only parties; check the room's relationship with alcohol first.

7. Recipe Kits

Everything needed for one dish or drink: Moscow Mule kit, taco night box, pancake breakfast stack.

8. As Seen on TV

Only gadgets with dramatic infomercial energy. Every unwrap gets a re-enacted ad read.

9. Thrifted Treasures

Vintage plates, retro glassware, forgotten board games. The $4 find that's better than anything new.

10. Comfy & Cozy

Blankets, socks, slippers, candles, cocoa. The office-safe theme that never produces a single complaint.

11. Hot Sauce Roulette

Every gift is sauce, spice, or snack with heat. Include milk. Trust us.

12. Book Swap

A book you love (gently used is fine) plus a note on why. Quiet groups discover they have opinions.

13. Retro Rewind

Only stuff from a declared decade. 90s theme: Tamagotchis, era candy, VHS-adjacent novelties.

14. Pet Distribution System

Gifts must be pet-themed or pet-usable. Winners photograph their pets with the loot for the group chat.

15. The Mixtape

Gift must include a hand-made playlist that matches it. The most effortful theme; the stories last longest.

Themes FAQ

What is a themed white elephant?

Same rules, curated pool: every guest brings a gift matching an announced theme instead of an open pool. It eliminates the gag-vs-serious mismatch that ruins open exchanges.

How do I announce the theme?

In the invite, with the budget, in one line: "White elephant, $20 cap, theme: thrifted treasures." State whether gifts skew gag or genuinely good within the theme.

What if someone ignores the theme?

Option A: their gift plays anyway. Option B (better): keep one spare themed gift on hand and swap it in before the game. Never let a rule-breaker's gift become the thing everyone remembers.

Best theme for a first-time group?

Dollar Store Mayhem — the low cap removes all gift anxiety, and the absurdity does the icebreaking for you.

Can a theme work with the Left-Right game?

Perfectly — a themed pool passes around the circle just as happily. See the Left-Right game rules.
