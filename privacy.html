Privacy Policy

What Stolen Elephants collects, how it’s used and the choices you have.

1. Who we are

This policy explains what personal information Stolen Elephants collects, how we use it and what choices you have. Stolen Elephants is operated by BOBO WEB CO LLC, a Texas company doing business as Stolen Elephants. It covers www.stolenelephants.com (the guide) and play.stolenelephants.com (the game app). Questions: Support@stolenelephants.com.

2. What we collect

In the game app

  • Account details: your display name and a PIN (stored only in hashed form).
  • Optional contact details: your email address, if you give one for turn alerts and game emails.
  • Delivery details: a shipping address (or an in-person hand-off note) that you enter at account setup so gifts can reach you. You may use an address other than your home address, such as a work address or a friend’s, but it can’t be a P.O. Box. Whatever you enter will be disclosed as described below.
  • Game content: your gift photos, videos and product links, thank-you videos, comments, votes, and what happens in the game (opens, steals and turn order).
  • Host information: game settings, the invite list if you invite players by email, and schedule and time-zone choices.
  • Sign-in cookie: a cookie that keeps you signed in to a game on your device for up to 90 days. It’s needed for the app to work and isn’t used for advertising.
  • Technical data: like most web services, our servers handle your IP address and browser details to deliver pages and limit abuse such as repeated PIN attempts.

When you pay

Payments are handled by Stripe. Your card details go to Stripe, not to us. We receive payment confirmation, the amount, the plan and limited billing details such as your name, email and the last four digits of the card. We never see or store your full card number.

On the guide website

The guide pages don’t ask you to create an account, and we don’t run advertising or analytics trackers on them. If you email us, we keep your message and address so we can reply.

3. How we use it

  • To run games: draw turns, reveal and steal gifts, and show comments, votes and results.
  • To send game emails you or your host have turned on: invites, welcome, turn alerts, the starting order, reminders and the end-of-game summary. Players can opt out under “My address & alerts” in the app.
  • To disclose your delivery address to the participant who owes you a gift, and only after the game ends, so they can deliver it.
  • To process payments and send receipts, prevent fraud and abuse, keep the service secure, and meet legal obligations.
  • To answer support requests and improve the service.

We don’t sell your personal information and we don’t use it for third-party advertising.

4. Who sees it

  • Other players and the host see what the game shows them: names, opened gifts, comments and votes.
  • The player who owes you a gift sees your delivery address. At the end of the game, the address you entered is disclosed to the participant who must send or deliver your gift, and only to that participant, so they can fulfill the delivery. By entering an address you agree to this disclosure. Once shared, we can’t control what that participant does with it, so only enter an address you’re comfortable giving them.
  • Service providers that help us run the service: Stripe (payments); our hosting provider, Fly.io; Cloudflare, which stores our database backups; and an email delivery provider that sends game emails. They may only use the data to provide their services to us. The app also loads the Baloo 2 font from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google when you use it.
  • Legal reasons: we may disclose information if required by law or to protect rights, safety and the service.
  • Business changes: if the business is sold or reorganized, information may transfer to the new owner under this policy.

5. Retention and your choices

  • We keep game data as long as it’s needed to run the service and for the legitimate purposes above, then delete or anonymize it.
  • You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information by emailing Support@stolenelephants.com. We’ll respond within a reasonable time, generally within 45 days. We may keep limited records we’re required to keep, such as payment records.
  • Residents of some states, including Texas, have rights to access, correct, delete and obtain a copy of their data and to opt out of the sale or targeted-advertising use of it. We don’t sell data or do targeted advertising. To use your rights, email us; if we deny a request you may appeal by replying to our response.
  • Hosts and players can remove comments and players within a game as described in the app.

6. Children

The service is not for children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, email Support@stolenelephants.com and we’ll delete it.

7. Security

We use reasonable safeguards, including hashed PINs, encrypted connections and access limits. No system is perfectly secure, so we can’t guarantee absolute security.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date above will change, and for significant changes we’ll make the update visible on the site.
