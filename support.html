🛠 Creating a game

For hosts: setting up, plans, invites and changes.

How do I start a game?

Go to play.stolenelephants.com and tap Host a game. Three quick steps: 1 · Plan (Free, Party or Business), 2 · Your game (a name, the start date and time on the calendar, an optional note to your players, a suggested gift budget and the rules), and 3 · You (you play too, so you get a name, PIN, email and shipping address like everyone else). Five minutes, tops. I’ve timed it. With my trunk.

Which plan should I pick?

Free holds up to 8 players. Party ($19 per event) holds up to 40 and adds gift and thank-you videos, a party photo gallery with comments, and your own logo. Business ($99 per event) holds up to 150 with longer videos. Not sure? Start Free. You can upgrade any time before the game starts, if your group outgrows the limit or you want the extras, and you only pay the difference. See Pricing.

How do I invite people?

Once the game exists, tap Invite at the top to share the link, or read out the game code (it’s the big one). You can also send email invites from the lobby. Anyone with the link can ask to join, and you can remove anyone who wandered in from the wrong party.

Can I change the start time, rules or budget later?

Yes. Until the game starts, the host can change the start time, steal limits, the note to players and the suggested budget from the lobby, and send the update to everyone. Gifts lock 10 minutes before the scheduled start, so herd your stragglers before then.

How do I pay for an upgrade, and can I get a refund?

Upgrades go through secure Stripe checkout, plus any sales tax. Changed your mind? You get a full refund until the game starts: email Support@stolenelephants.com with your game code. After the game starts, the plan is locked in.

How do I add my company or family logo?

On Party and Business games, the host can upload a JPEG or PNG (up to 2 MB) from the Game plan card in the lobby. It appears at the top of the game and in game emails. Party and Business games can add their own logo; Free games get my face instead. You’re welcome.

🙋 Joining a game

For players: signing up, your address and adding your gift.

I got a link. Now what?

Open it. That’s it. (If you only have a code, go to play.stolenelephants.com, tap Join with a code and type it in.) Pick a name and a 4 to 8 digit PIN, add your email for turn alerts, and enter the address where your gift should go.

Why do you need my address? Who sees it?

So the person who ends up owing you a gift can actually send it. Your address is shown to that one person only, and only after the game ends. Nobody else sees it, we don’t sell it, and it’s deleted with the game, 15 days after the game ends. You can use a work address or a friend’s, but not a P.O. Box.

How do I add my gift?
In the lobby, choose one of three ways:
  • I have it: snap up to 4 photos (or a short video on Party and Business games).
  • I found it online: paste the product link and tap Check link. We fill in the name and pictures for you.
  • I need ideas: browse Ellie’s gift ideas list, filter by price and category, and tap Make this my White Elephant.
Your gift stays hidden from everyone until someone opens it in the game. No peeking. I’m watching.
I forgot my PIN or I’m on a new phone.

Open the invite link again and choose Sign back in with the same name and PIN. If the PIN has truly left the building, ask your host, or write to me at ellie@stolenelephants.com with the game code and your player name.

The game says it’s full.

The host’s plan has hit its player limit (8 on Free, 40 on Party). The host gets an email with a one-tap upgrade. Give them a friendly nudge. Or an unfriendly one. I don’t judge.

Can I change my gift after I’ve added it?

Yes, right up until gifts lock 10 minutes before the scheduled start.

The icons you’ll see in the game, and what they mean.

🦹Steal

Game play questions

Whose turn is it?

The order is drawn at random when the host starts the game. The player whose turn it is gets a big banner (and an email, if alerts are on). Everyone else gets to watch and heckle.

What can I do on my turn?

Either 🎀 open a wrapped gift or 🦹 steal one that’s already open. If someone steals from you, you go again right away: open a new one or steal from someone else.

Can I steal my gift right back?

Not immediately, if the host kept the “no immediate steal-back” rule on (it’s on by default). Wait a turn, then pounce.

When does a gift lock?

After it’s been stolen the maximum number of times the host set (3 by default). Locked gifts show 🔒 and can’t be stolen again.

What’s the final swap?

If the host turned it on, player #1, who opened first and never got to steal, gets one last chance at the end: swap for any unlocked gift, or keep what they have.

Do we all have to be online at the same time?

Nope. Play live on a video call, or let the game run over a few days with a pick timer and active hours. Emails tell each player when it’s their turn.

📦 After the game

Sending gifts, thank-yous and what happens to your data.

What do I do when the game ends?

The end screen and your final email lead with 📦 the gift you send: what it is, who it goes to, their shipping address and, if you found it online, a 🛒 link to order it. You’ll also see what’s coming your way. Send or order your gift promptly; elephants never forget, and neither do your coworkers.

Can I say thank you?

On Party and Business games, whoever ends up with a gift can record a short 🎥 thank-you video for the person who brought it. They have two weeks after the game ends.

How long do photos, addresses and videos stick around?

A finished game, with its addresses, gifts, gallery photos and videos, is permanently deleted 15 days after it ends (a game that never finishes goes after 30 days without activity). The host can delete it sooner. Save the summary if you want a keepsake.

My gift never arrived. Help?

Start with the person who owed it to you; the end screen shows who that is. We coordinate the game, but gifts are sent between players and retailers, so we can’t ship or replace them. If something seems off, write to me at ellie@stolenelephants.com and I’ll see what I can do.

Can I play again next year?

Please do. Tick “Keep me posted” when you give your email and I’ll send a reminder before the season gets away from you. A few emails a year, unsubscribe any time.

Still stuck? Email Ellie at ellie@stolenelephants.com. Billing and refunds: Support@stolenelephants.com. Include your game code and we’ll sort it out. Follow along at @stolenelephants.
