Stolen Elephants Pricing

Three tiers, one simple rule: you pay per event, never per seat and never per month. A free game for up to 8 friends, $19 for the department, $99 for the whole company.

Per Event · No Subscriptions

Quick answer

Stolen Elephants charges per event, never per seat or per month. The Free plan covers up to 8 players for $0, Party is $19 per event for up to 30 players, and Business is $99 per event for up to 150 players. All plans include the rulebook engine, which handles draws, steals, Three-Owner locks, and the final swap.

Common pricing questions

Short answers; the FAQ has more.

Is it per person? No — per event. A 30-player Party event costs $19 total, not $19 a head.

Is there a subscription? No. Pay when you host an event; play free under 8 players forever.

Can we upgrade after we’ve started setting up? Yes. Until the game starts, the host can upgrade from inside the game, pay for the new tier by card, and invite the rest of the floor. Once the game starts, the plan is locked in.

Stolen Elephants Pricing — FAQ

The questions this page always raises.

How much does Stolen Elephants cost?

Free for up to 8 players. Party tier is $19 per event for 9–30 players, and Business tier is $99 per event for 41–150 players. Always per event — never per person or per month.

Is there a free plan?

Yes — the full rulebook engine (draw, steals, Three-Owner Rule locks, final swap, live or async) is free for groups up to 8 players, with no time limit.

What’s the difference between Party and Business tiers?

Party covers 9–30 players at $19 per event, Business covers 41–150 at $99. Both include gift and thank-you videos and a party photo gallery where players post pictures and comment to each other and the host; Business allows longer videos (45 seconds instead of 30). Party and Business games can add their own logo.

What is the party photo gallery?

On Party and Business games, every player can post photos (ugly sweaters, wrapping crimes, the face of someone whose gift just got stolen) to a shared gallery inside the game. Anyone in the game can comment, and the host can remove anything that shouldn’t be there. The gallery is deleted with the rest of the game, 15 days after it ends.
