White Elephant Invitation Wording

Nine invitation templates for every flavor of game — family, office, funny, classroom, async, premium — plus the subject lines and the details that keep guests from texting you questions all week.

Copy · Paste · Send

Quick answer

A good white elephant invitation states the date, time, place, budget, and the basic rules so guests don't text you questions all week. This page gives nine ready-to-use templates, including family, office, funny, classroom, async, and premium versions, plus subject lines and a details checklist.

Every template below is copy-paste ready: swap the [BRACKETS], keep the rules line (it prevents 90% of day-of questions), and send. Rule Sheet handouts for printing, email, and text live one click away in the resources.

The templates

Seven invites + two subject lines + one checklist.

The classic family one

FAMILY

One line of logistics, one line of rules, one line of warning.

You’re invited to the [FAMILY NAME] White Elephant! [DATE] at [TIME], [ADDRESS]. Bring one wrapped gift, around $[AMOUNT]. Rules are simple: three owners and it locks, no steal-backs, Player 1 gets the final swap. The app tracks everything — join at [EVENT LINK]. Last year’s champ, prepare to be dethroned.

The office one

OFFICE

HR-friendly, calendar-ready, zero ambiguity about the cap.

Our team’s White Elephant is [DATE] at [TIME] in [LOCATION / VIDEO LINK]. Budget: $[AMOUNT] — firm, per policy and per tradition. Async-friendly: turns land in your inbox, steal on your own schedule all week. Join: [EVENT LINK]. Questions? The rulebook: stolenelephants.com/white-elephant-rules

The funny one

FUNNY

For groups that choose their gift exchange by how hard they can laugh.

🐘 WHITE ELEPHANT: [DATE], [TIME], [PLACE]. Bring a $[AMOUNT] gift you’ll pretend you didn’t buy yourself. Three owners and it locks. No crying (out loud). Uncle Randall’s 2016 masterpiece is eligible to return. Join: [EVENT LINK]

The classroom one

CLASSROOM

Goes home in a backpack; survives a parent’s second read.

Dear families — on [DATE], our class will hold a Book Exchange! Each student brings one wrapped book in good condition (new or like-new). We’ll unwrap together in class and everyone takes home a “new” book. Please keep it under $10 and avoid food items. Note by [DATE] if you’d like a book provided instead.

The virtual / async one

VIRTUAL

For teams across time zones — the game fits the week, not the calendar.

Our White Elephant is async this year: [START DAY] to [END DAY]. One gift, $[AMOUNT], one link — join at [EVENT LINK] and take your turn when your workday allows. Hospital beds, airports, and other time zones are all first-class players here. Finale chain: [OPTIONAL VIDEO CALL, DAY/TIME].

The premium one

PREMIUM

For the night you’re all feeling generous — or rich, or both.

You’re cordially invited to a Premium White Elephant: [DATE], [TIME], [ADDRESS]. This year’s cap is $[AMOUNT] — think French presses, not fingernail clippers. Same rules, finer prizes. Dress code: better than last year. Join: [EVENT LINK]

The day-of reminder text

REMINDER

Two hours out. Works for any of the above.

⏰ Tonight: white elephant at [TIME], [PLACE]. Bring your $[AMOUNT] gift. Numbers drawn at [TIME] sharp — late = last pick. Rules refresher: stolenelephants.com/white-elephant-rules

What every invite needs

The five details that prevent the 9 p.m. panic text.

  1. Date, time, place — or the async window start/end, if the game runs all week.
  2. The price cap, stated as firm — '$25' invites interpretation; 'cap: $25, firm' doesn't.
  3. One wrapped gift — say 'wrapped'; someone will otherwise bring a gift bag with a bow on it and a receipt inside.
  4. The rules line or a link — 'three owners and it locks; no steal-backs; final swap for Player 1' or just link the rulebook.
  5. The event link — join before the party, not during the draw.

White Elephant Invitation Wording — FAQ

The questions this page always raises.

What should a white elephant invitation say?

Date, time, place, the firm price cap, 'one wrapped gift', the event link, and one line of rules ('three owners and it locks'). Everything else is decoration.

How far in advance should you send white elephant invites?

Two to three weeks for family and office games — enough shopping runway without the invite getting buried. A reminder text the day of rescues the rest.

How do you word a virtual white elephant invitation?

State the async window instead of a single time (‘play anytime between Monday and Thursday’), the cap, and the join link. Add the finale call time if you’re booking one.

What is a fun white elephant invite?

Name the tradition (‘Uncle Randall’s 2016 masterpiece is eligible to return’), warn about the lock rule, and keep the logistics to three lines. The funny template above does all three.
