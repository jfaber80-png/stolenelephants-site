Why $10 gifts steal anyway

The cheap-gift paradox.

Steal appeal isn’t price — it’s universality plus a story. A tiny cassette dispenser out-steals a $40 gadget because everyone understands it instantly and everyone knows who it should live with. Under $10, aim for: useful-on-a-desk, instantly nostalgic, or edible-spectacular. The five gift types explains the taxonomy; the 38-finds list covers the $10–$25 range.
